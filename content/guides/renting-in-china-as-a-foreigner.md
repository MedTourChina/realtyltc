:::answer
One-year lease, one to three months deposit, agency fee around one month's rent. Register at the police station within 24 hours of moving in, and keep the registration slip — you will need it repeatedly.
:::

Renting is how most foreign buyers should start in China, and it is also where they learn the local rules cheaply. The lease itself is usually a standard municipal form with a schedule of additions, and the additions are where the arguments later come from.

## The money

| Item | Typical amount | Paid to | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Rent | Monthly, in advance | Landlord | Quarterly payment is also common |
| Deposit | 1–3 months | Landlord | Should be refundable, in writing |
| Agency fee | 35–100% of one month | Agency | Often split between landlord and tenant |
| Management fee | ¥3–15/m² per month | Property management | Confirm who pays; it is negotiable |
| Utilities | Metered | Utility companies | Water, electricity, gas, heating |
| Internet | ¥100–200/month | Provider | Usually tenant's arrangement |

Heating is billed separately in northern cities and is a real annual cost, not a rounding error. Confirm whether it is included before you compare two apartments.

## The twelve clauses that matter

:::checklist
- **Deposit return conditions**, with a deadline in days and a deduction standard
- **Early termination**, by either side, and the notice period
- **Repair responsibility** — what the landlord fixes, what you fix, and the threshold
- **Rent increase** — whether it is fixed for the term, and what happens on renewal
- **Subletting and guests** — the clause that catches people hosting a partner or family
- **Registration cooperation** — the landlord's obligation to support your police registration
- **Property management fees** and who bears them
- **Furniture inventory**, itemised, with condition notes
- **Alterations**, and whether you may drill, paint or install air conditioning
- **Landlord's right of entry**, and the notice required
- **Dispute resolution**, and the jurisdiction
- **Early exit on relocation**, if your employer might move you
:::

:::warn
The subletting and guest clauses are the two most frequently breached by foreign tenants, usually without knowing it. Hosting a long-term partner, or letting a colleague stay for a month, can be a contractual breach that costs you the deposit. Read the clause, then ask for written permission if you need it.
:::

## The registration you must not skip

Foreign nationals must complete residence registration (住宿登记) at the local police station after moving in. Hotels do it automatically; a private lease does not.

Do it within 24 hours of arrival, and again after any address change. You will need your passport, visa or residence permit, the lease, and the landlord's ID and property certificate.

This is not bureaucratic decoration. A missing registration can:

- Complicate a residence permit renewal
- Delay a bank account opening
- Block a school application for your child
- Create a problem when you leave the country and return

:::note
Ask the landlord to accompany you, or to provide a signed authorisation and a copy of the property certificate. Many landlords have never done this for a foreign tenant and will need prompting. It is worth a small rent concession to get their cooperation in writing before you sign.
:::

## What "furnished" means here

Furnished in China usually means the landlord has furnished it to a standard they consider adequate, which is not the same as your standard. Check specifically:

- Air conditioning in every bedroom, and its age
- Water pressure, especially on upper floors of older buildings
- Whether the kitchen has an oven (often not)
- Bed size and mattress condition
- Whether the internet line is already installed and whose name it is in

## How to not be cheated

**Duplicate listings** are the standard tactic of the low-quality end of the agency market: an attractive unit that does not exist, used to collect your phone number. Insist on a viewing of the specific unit, and check that the address on the listing matches the address you visit.

**Phantom landlords** are rarer but real. Verify the name on the property certificate against the name on the lease, and never pay a deposit before you have seen both.

**Deposit confiscation** is the most common dispute at exit. Photograph everything at move-in, have the inventory signed and dated, and keep the condition report. A deposit dispute without a move-in record is a deposit you lose.

## The lease term and your visa

A one-year lease aligns with most residence permits, and landlords prefer it. If your assignment is shorter, say so up front — a six-month lease with a two-month penalty for early exit may still be cheaper than a twelve-month commitment you have to break.

If you are planning to buy, a twelve-month lease is not wasted time. It is the cheapest possible due diligence on a district, and it is the period during which you accumulate the employment record that the purchase eligibility check will require.
