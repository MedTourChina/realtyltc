## Not advice

Nothing here is legal, tax, financial or investment advice, and nothing here creates a professional relationship of any kind. The site describes how a process generally works in China; it cannot tell you how that process will treat your particular documents, your particular municipality or your particular marriage.

Where a decision has legal or tax consequences — ownership structures, inheritance, the repatriation of sale proceeds, the tax treatment of a specific transaction — consult a licensed professional in the relevant jurisdiction.

## Accuracy and currency

Chinese property rules are set at national, municipal and sometimes district level, and they change with little notice. Figures on this site are indicative ranges with a stated date, not guarantees. Yields, prices and costs are compiled from public sources and are not appraisals.

Every page shows the date it was last reviewed. A page reviewed more than six months ago may no longer be accurate even though it is still published, and the site will say so where that is the case. If you find an error, please report it.
