:::answer
There is nothing to opt out of. This site sets no cookies, runs no analytics, has no forms, no accounts and no newsletter. It is a set of static pages served from a content delivery network.
:::

## What we collect

**Nothing.** Not an email address, not a device identifier, not a referring page, not a click.

There is no form on this site to submit, so there is no information to receive. There is no account system, so there is no profile. There is no analytics script, so there is no behavioural record.

## Cookies and local storage

No cookies are set by this site. The three calculators run entirely in your browser using JavaScript; their inputs are not stored anywhere and are discarded when you close the tab.

The decision sheet is the one exception, and it uses local storage rather than a cookie. Ticking a box writes that box's identifier into your browser's `localStorage` under the key `ltc-decision-sheet-v1`, so your progress survives a refresh or a closed tab. That record never leaves your browser: there is no server here to receive it, no account to attach it to, and no script from another domain that could read it. Pressing "Reset" on the page, or clearing site data in your browser, deletes it.

## Third-party requests

Pages on this site load one stylesheet and one script, both from this domain. There are no fonts, no trackers, no pixels, no embedded videos, no social widgets and no advertising tags. If you follow a link to another site, that site's practices apply once you arrive.

## Server logs

Like any website, the hosting provider records standard request information — IP address, requested URL, timestamp, user agent — in order to deliver the page and defend against abuse. This is infrastructure-level logging, not profiling: we do not build a picture of individual readers, do not combine those logs with anything else, and do not have a mechanism to identify you from them.

## If you email us

The site publishes a single address for factual corrections. If you write to it, your message and address exist in an inbox, and nowhere else. They are not added to a list, not shared, and not used for any purpose other than reading and replying. You can ask for the message to be deleted at any time.

## Children

The site is written for adults making property decisions and is not directed at anyone under eighteen.

## Changes

If this ever changes — if a contact form is added, or a privacy-respecting analytics tool is introduced — this page will be updated before that happens, and the change will be described plainly rather than buried.
